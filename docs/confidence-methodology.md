# Confidence methodology

Default weights are source reliability 20%; transaction, identity, and ownership certainty 15% each; consideration and completion certainty 10% each; tax certainty, deployment coverage, and recency 5% each.

Labels are very high (90–100), high (80–89), moderate (65–79), low (50–64), and insufficient for default publication (below 50). Private events require at least 65 plus analyst approval; default rankings require at least 65.
